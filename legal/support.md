## 1. Getting Help with GrantHarbor

The fastest way to reach a human is email: [support@emberinformation.com](mailto:support@emberinformation.com?subject=GrantHarbor%20support). We aim to reply within 48 hours.

To report a bug from inside the app, use the 🦗 in Settings — it prefills your app version and platform so we can reproduce it faster.

## 2. Your Data & Deletion Requests

GrantHarbor keeps your data on your device — to delete it, remove it in the app or uninstall. We hold nothing to delete on our end.

## 3. Known Issues & Updates

Material changes and known issues are surfaced in the app. GrantHarbor is version 1.2, effective September 2026.
