# GrantGuard — "Beat These" (differentiation spec)

Source: 100-idea demand map — nonprofit/small-org ops pains (#50 nonprofit tools, #22 solo
CRM, #12 micro-agency portal). GrantGuard = grant tracking for small orgs; Pro = ingestion
agent. Incumbents' failures → our targets.

## Complaints to BEAT
| Incumbent failure | GrantGuard answer |
|---|---|
| **Expensive nonprofit/per-seat tiers** | Honest pricing; a genuinely useful free/Basic tier (manual import), Pro is the ingestion agent — pay for the automation, not for existing. |
| **Complex enterprise UI / heavy training** | One primary action per screen (EIS_APP_STANDARDS); a small org is productive day one. |
| **Weak reporting for solo/small orgs** | Reports built for a 1–3 person shop, not a grants department. |
| **Duplicate records** | Dedup on ingest (the Pro agent's job); one grant, one record. |
| **Opaque pricing / surprise paywalls** | Pricing shown plainly; the paywall gates the *agent*, never your own data. |
| **Noisy notifications** | Deadline/match alerts that matter; no notification spam. |
| **Data lock-in / weak export** | Exportable, deletable, local-first — your grant pipeline is yours. |

## The wedge (positioning)
For the **1–3 person nonprofit or founder chasing grants** who **can't afford a grants
department or an enterprise suite**, GrantGuard is a **calm grant tracker** where **the agent
finds the opportunities so nothing slips a deadline** — unlike the incumbents, **it has an
honest free tier, no enterprise bloat, and never locks up your data.**

Reassuring-precision voice (professional/financial category). Feed into the bible + the
Upgrade page copy + Hunny Do ship checklist.
