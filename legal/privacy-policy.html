1. Our Commitment

Ember Information Systems LLC builds GrantHarbor on a simple promise: your data is yours. We do not sell your personal data. We do not engage in behavioral advertising. We do not build advertising profiles.

This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it.

3. How We Use Data

We use your data solely to operate the Service: to authenticate you, to store and display the content you create, to process payments where applicable, and to debug and improve reliability. We do not use it for any purpose you would not reasonably expect.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only where strictly necessary to run the Service, or where legally compelled:

  • Service providers strictly bound to operate the Service on our behalf
  • Authorities, only where required by valid legal process

5. Data Retention

We retain nothing, because nothing reaches us. Your files stay on your device and are kept or deleted entirely by you.

6. Your Rights

These rights exist wherever a company holds your personal data. In this application they are already satisfied by design, because we hold none of it:

  • Access — everything is in your own files, on your own device
  • Correction — edit your files directly; we have no copy to correct
  • Erasure — delete the files, or uninstall. There is no account and nothing held elsewhere
  • Portability — your data never left a standard file format to begin with
  • Restriction & Objection — limit or object to certain processing

To exercise any of these, or to ask a question, contact support@emberinformation.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Security

There is no server to breach and no database of users to leak, because neither exists. The security of your files is the security of your own device — so keep it locked and backed up, and be as careful with the files you export as you would with the originals.

8. Local-First by Design

GrantHarbor is built local-first: wherever possible, your data lives on your device or your own instance rather than a central server we mine. This is a deliberate architectural choice, not a marketing line.

9. Children’s Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Outbound Connections

GrantHarbor connects to two external services, both operated by the U.S. federal government and both public: the Grants.gov search API (the source of the open funding opportunities the app finds for you) and the USASpending.gov award API (used by Pro’s “who has won funding like this” precedent lookup). No other servers are contacted for grant data.

These requests are identical for every user of the app. They contain no account information, no keywords, no identifiers, and no contents of your records — the app downloads the public lists and then compares them against your keywords on your own device. Neither service can tell what you are looking for, or that the request came from you rather than any other user.

This connection is only made when you run a search from the Discover page. It is never automatic, and the rest of the app works with no network connection at all.

11. Connectivity & Consent

GrantHarbor is local-first: by default, your records stay on your device. Some optional features need to connect to outside services to work — for example email, bug reports, and submitting or exporting your applications.

Pro unlocks the ingestion agent, which scans open federal opportunities (via the public APIs in §10) and matches them against your keywords on your own device — your keywords never leave it, and Pro does not upload or sync your records. Pro is purchased on our website (emberinformation.shop); payment is handled by our payment processor (Stripe), and Ember Information Systems never sees your card details. Your Pro licence — the email it was issued to, plus the key — is stored on your device and verified offline; there is no licence server to check in with.

Before any feature transmits your data, creates an account, or collects information, Ember Information Systems will tell you in plain language and let you choose whether to turn it on — except where the law requires otherwise.
